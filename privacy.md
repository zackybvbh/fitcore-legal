# Fitcore — Privacy Policy / Политика конфиденциальности

**Last updated: September 29, 2026**

Developer / Разработчик: **ZackysStudio**
App / Приложение: **Fitcore — workout & nutrition tracker**
Contact / Контакты: **support.zackysstudio@gmail.com**

> This document describes the app's actual data practices but is **not legal advice**;
> have it reviewed by a qualified professional before you rely on it.

This Privacy Policy explains how **Fitcore** ("the App", "we", "us"), published by
**ZackysStudio**, handles information when you use the App. The App is a workout planning,
nutrition tracking and AI fitness assistant app for a general audience. **It is not directed
to children under 16** (or the equivalent minimum age in your country).

By using the App you agree to this Policy.

---

## 1. Summary

- To use the App you create an account with an **email and password**, or by **signing in
  with Google**.
- We store your **profile and health data** (weight, height, calorie/macro targets, goal,
  workouts, nutrition log) on a server that **we operate ourselves**.
- The App's **food-scanning** and **AI assistant** features send your photos and messages to
  **Google's Gemini API** for processing.
- If you purchase a **Premium** subscription, it is billed through **Google Play / the App
  Store**, and subscription status is confirmed through **RevenueCat**.
- Workout reminders are scheduled **locally on your device** and are never sent to us.
- We do **not** sell your personal information, and we do **not** show ads.
- Data exchanged between the App and our servers or providers is protected **in transit using
  encryption (HTTPS/TLS)**.

---

## 2. Information we and our providers process

### 2.1 Account data

When you register with an email and password, or sign in with Google, we process your
**email address**. Passwords are stored **hashed** and are never accessible to us in plain
text. If you sign in with Google, we also receive the **name and profile picture** associated
with your Google account.

### 2.2 Health, nutrition and workout data

To provide the App's core features, we store: your **weight, height, target calories and
macros** (protein/fat/carbs), your **goal** (cutting/maintenance/bulking), your **weekly
workout target**, your **workout history and logged exercises**, and your **nutrition log
entries**. This data is stored on our server so that it can sync across your devices.

### 2.3 Photos and food scanning (Google Gemini API)

If you use the food-scanning feature, the photo of your meal is sent to **Google's Gemini
API** to recognize its composition and estimate its calories and macros. Google processes the
image content and related technical data as an independent data processor. See:
https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms

### 2.4 AI assistant conversations (Google Gemini API)

Messages you send to the built-in AI workout and nutrition assistant, together with relevant
profile context needed to answer your question (such as your goals or recent workouts), are
sent to **Google's Gemini API** to generate a response.

### 2.5 Subscriptions and purchases (RevenueCat & app store billing)

If you buy a **Premium** subscription, the transaction is handled by **Google Play Billing**
or the **App Store**. We use **RevenueCat** to validate and manage your subscription status.
RevenueCat processes a purchase token and an app-user identifier to confirm whether your
subscription is active. **We never receive or store your full payment card details** — those
are handled by the app store. See: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

### 2.6 Local reminders and notifications

The App can schedule reminders about workouts and meals at times you choose. These reminders
are scheduled **entirely on your device** using the device's own notification system; no
reminder content or schedule is sent to us.

### 2.7 On-device data

Some data (such as cached content and app settings) is stored locally on your device to
support a responsive, partly offline experience.

### 2.8 Server and technical logs

Our server, which we operate ourselves, processes standard technical data with every request
needed to serve it and keep it secure — such as your **IP address**, device type, and app
version.

## 3. How we use information

We (and our providers, for their own described purposes) use the information to:

- create and operate your account, and sync your data across your devices;
- calculate your personalized calorie and macro targets and show your progress;
- power the photo-based food recognition feature and the AI assistant;
- process and validate your Premium subscription;
- keep the service secure, prevent abuse, and provide customer support.

We do **not** sell your personal information, and we do **not** use your data for
advertising.

---

## 4. Permissions the App requests

- **Internet** — to sync your account and use the online features described above.
- **Camera and photo library** — to take or select a photo of your food for the scanning
  feature.
- **Notifications** — to show you the reminders you schedule.
- **Exact alarms** (`SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM` on Android) — so reminders you schedule fire at the
  exact time you chose, including while the device is idle.
- **Boot completed** — so your scheduled reminders are re-armed after your device restarts.

## 5. Data sharing

We share data only with the providers named above, and only as needed to provide the App's
features:

- **Google Gemini API** processes photos and messages you submit to the food-scanning and AI
  assistant features, as an independent data processor.
- **Google Sign-In** processes your email, name and profile picture if you choose that
  sign-in method.
- **RevenueCat** and the relevant **app store (Google Play / App Store)** process your
  subscription and purchase data.

We do not use any advertising or analytics SDKs, and our core database runs on
infrastructure that we operate directly rather than a third-party cloud database provider.

We may also disclose information if required by law. We do **not** sell your personal
information.
